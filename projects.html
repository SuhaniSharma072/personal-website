Patent Innovations

A three-part system that predicts hydroplaning before it happens. When the car is parked, fender-mounted sensors deploy and use AR to measure the tread depth of each tire, then retract so mud and rocks can't reach the lens. While driving, a LiDAR sensor at the front scans the road ahead for standing water, telling a wide flooded stretch apart from a small puddle, and tracks the car's speed. An AI integrator combines tread depth, water, and speed into a risk score.

For everyday drivers, a Bluetooth sticker on the dashboard flashes and plays an audio warning, and announces which tire is worn at startup. In a self-driving car, the system slows the vehicle down automatically.

Brings blind-spot monitoring to older cars by repurposing the shark fin antenna housing on the roof. Inside, LiDAR scanners and radar build a live 3D picture of the surroundings and send it to a center console that shows the driver nearby vehicles, their speed, and distance. It installs in seconds on a heavy-duty magnetic base without changing the car's aerodynamics, even on cars without a shark fin, and optional cameras add a second check in bad weather.
