  • ✦

    Web Developer & Site Manager, Vakrangee Traders

    My grandfather started a textile business in his small town in India, which my maternal uncle runs today. At a time when many families in their town wouldn't let their daughters work, he pushed for my mom to go into tech, spending money he could have put back into his own business. After he passed away, the business was never the same. It still runs the traditional way, and it has been losing money because payments and orders were never streamlined. I owe the lifestyle I have to his choice, so I built and manage a B2B e-commerce site for the business with Next.js and Tailwind, connecting it to both wholesale and individual buyers through a Shopify-backed catalog, Razorpay payments, and automated order emails. It also has a "Text on WhatsApp" button, since WhatsApp is the most used way to communicate in India.
